💡 Visitor Tips
Arrive early

The park is large. Arriving early gives you more time to explore different attractions comfortably.

Keep your ticket safe

Some attractions inside the park may require separate tickets.

Follow wildlife safety rules

Never feed, disturb or approach wild animals. Follow instructions from park staff.

⚠️ Important: Ticket prices, opening hours and attraction availability may change. Please confirm the latest price and availability at the park ticket counter before your visit.
